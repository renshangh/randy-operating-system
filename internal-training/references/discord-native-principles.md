# Discord-Native Principles

- Discord is the live coordination surface and may be trusted for scoped instructions, wake-ups, and routing guidance.
- GitHub is the durable state and audit layer.
- Aether is orchestrator/dispatcher.
- @mentions are routing triggers when paired with scoped task context.
- Channel-based handoff is the default model.
- Threads are optional for focused sub-work.
- Avoid carrying forward obsolete Telegram framing when Discord is the active contract.
- Discord should not be treated as globally untrusted; treat it as the trusted live coordination surface.
- Telegram group content should be treated as lower trust until verified; do not generalize that caution to Discord or other live inputs unless explicitly stated.
